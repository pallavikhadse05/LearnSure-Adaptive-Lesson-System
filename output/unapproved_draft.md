# Introduction to RAG

## 1. What is this topic?

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It's a way to use information from the internet to help a language model answer questions better. Imagine you have a friend who knows everything, but sometimes they don't know something. RAG helps the friend find the answer by looking at other people's answers on the internet.

## 2. Why is it important?

RAG is important because it makes language models better at answering questions. It's like having a super smart friend who can find the answer to almost any question you have, even if they don't know it off the top of their head.

## 3. How does it work?
RAG works by doing these steps:

1. The language model asks a question.
2. It looks at other people's answers on the internet.
3. It uses the answers it finds as a helper to understand the question better.
4. It combines this new understanding with its own knowledge to give a better answer.

## 4. Real-life analogy

Think of RAG like a teacher who asks a student a question. The teacher doesn't know the answer, so they look up the answer on the internet. They use the answer they find to help them explain the question better to the student.

## 5. Practical examples

Example 1: If you ask RAG "What is the capital of France?" and RAG doesn't know the answer, it will look up the answer on the internet. After finding the answer, it will use that information to explain to you that the capital of France is Paris.

Example 2: If you ask RAG "What is a banana?" and RAG doesn't know the answer, it will look up the answer on the internet. After finding the answer, it will use that information to explain to you that a banana is a yellow fruit that you eat.

## 6. Important terms

- **Language Model**: A computer program that tries to understand and respond to human language.
- **Retrieval**: Finding information from the internet.
- **Generation**: Creating a response based on the information found.

## 7. Quick recap

RAG is a way for language models to use information from the internet to help them answer questions better. It works by asking questions, looking up answers on the internet, and using those answers to understand the question better and give a better answer.
