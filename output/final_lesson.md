# Lesson Title: Introduction to RAG

## Learning Objectives:
- Understand what RAG means.
- Know how RAG works.
- Be able to use RAG in simple tasks.

## Simple Introduction
RAG is a way to use different types of information to help make decisions. It's like asking for help from different people or sources to find the best answer.

## Explanation of the Topic
RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmentation-Generation. It's a method where you look for information (Retrieval), add to it (Augmentation), and then use it to create something new (Generation).

## Step-by-Step Working
1. **Find Information (Retrieval)**: Look for information in books, the internet, or ask someone.
2. **Add Information (Augmentation)**: Think about what you already know and add it to the information you found.
3. **Create Something New (Generation)**: Use the new information to make a decision or answer a question.

## Practical Example
Imagine you want to know what to wear for a party. You look up what's popular on the internet. Then you think about what you like and what's comfortable. Finally, you decide on a dress.

## Explanation of Technical Terms
- **Retrieval**: Finding information.
- **Augmentation**: Adding more information.
- **Generation**: Creating something new.

## Summary
RAG is a way to use different sources of information to help make decisions. It involves finding information, adding to it, and then using it to make a decision.
